## Key Verified Facts
- **Origin**: Established from Dr. Ernest Earl Lockhart's May 1957 paper (*The Soluble Solids in Beverage Coffee as an Index to Cup Quality*) at the Coffee Brewing Institute (CBI), later adopted and maintained by the Specialty Coffee Association (SCA).
- **Golden Cup Zone (Coffee Brewing Control Chart, 9 zones)**:
  - **Total Dissolved Solids (TDS / 濃度)**: **1.15% to 1.35%** (below 1.15% = weak; above 1.35% = strong). Measured with a refractometer.
  - **Extraction Yield / Percent Extraction (PE / 萃取率)**: **18% to 22%** (below 18% = under-extracted: sour, sharp, thin; above 22% = over-extracted: bitter, dry, astringent).
  - **Formula**: `PE (%) = (TDS × Brewed Coffee Mass) ÷ Dry Coffee Dose × 100`.
- **Operational Parameters (SCA)**:
  - **Coffee-to-Water Ratio**: **55 g/L ±10%** (49.5–60.5 g/L, roughly **1:18** by weight).
  - **Pour-Over Adaptation**: Recommended starting dose **15 g coffee to 250 ml water** (~1:16 to 1:17), total brew contact time **2.5–4 minutes**, with a bloom using ~2× coffee weight in water (30–40 ml for 15–20 g dose) for **30–45 seconds**.
  - **Water Temperature**: **93.0°C ±3°C (200°F ±5°F)** at point of contact.
  - **Water Quality (per S1)**: Odor-free, chlorine-free, calcium hardness **50–175 ppm CaCO₃**, alkalinity **40–70 ppm CaCO₃**, ideal pH **7.0**.
  - **Automatic Drip Contact Time**: 4–8 minutes (over 8 minutes = disqualification in SCA certification).
- **Modern Research Update (UC Davis Coffee Center, 2020–2023)**:
  - Cotter et al. (2020/2021, 118 black coffee drinkers) found preferred brews span **19–24% PE** and **1.1–1.3% TDS** with two distinct consumer clusters.
  - Batali, Frost, Guinard & Ristenpart (2023, *Journal of Food Science*) introduced a revised descriptive Sensory & Consumer Brewing Control Chart.
