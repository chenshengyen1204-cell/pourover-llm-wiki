## Key Verified Facts
- **Three Core Water Metrics**:
  - **TDS (Total Dissolved Solids)**: Sum of all dissolved substances (calcium, magnesium, sodium, potassium, bicarbonate, etc., per USGS).
  - **Hardness (硬度)**: Amount of dissolved **calcium and magnesium** ions. USGS categories (as CaCO₃): Soft (**0–60 mg/L**), Moderately hard (**61–120 mg/L**), Hard (**121–180 mg/L**), Very hard (**>180 mg/L**).
  - **Alkalinity (鹼度)**: Buffering capacity driven by bicarbonates, carbonates, and hydroxides — indicates how strongly water neutralizes coffee acids.
- **SCA Brewing-Water Standard (per S6)**:
  - **TDS**: Target **150 mg/L** (acceptable **75–250 mg/L**).
  - **Calcium Hardness**: Target **68 mg/L (4 grains)** (acceptable **17–85 mg/L**).
  - **Total Alkalinity**: Target **40 mg/L** (acceptable near 40 mg/L).
  - **pH**: Target **7.0** (acceptable **6.5–7.5**).
  - **Sodium**: Target **10 mg/L**.
  - **Total Chlorine**: Target **0 mg/L** (any chlorine is a red flag for flavor).
- **Red Flags & Practical Tip**:
  - Very hard water (>180 mg/L CaCO₃) causes kettle/equipment scale buildup.
  - TDS outside 75–250 mg/L or presence of chlorine sits outside SCA standards; home brewers can check their municipal annual water quality report first.
